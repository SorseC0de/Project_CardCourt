# SHOT audit, 2026-09-14

Every SHOT-boosting card, read out one by one, with the user's answers. Text is verbatim and
follows [card-text-style.md](card-text-style.md). SHOT is the badge figure. Anything a card's
row doesn't mention is unchanged.
