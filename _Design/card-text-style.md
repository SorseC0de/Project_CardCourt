# Card text style

The user's wording rules, set during the SHOT audit (2026-09-14). Every card follows them;
new card text is written to them first.

## Figures

- **SHOT lives on the badge.** A card's own SHOT figure is the icon, not a sentence of text.
- **Shot Clock numbers take a leading zero**: `Shot Clock -01`, `+01`, `03`.
- **"2X" replaces "Double"** — an icon: white text with a light blue bottom half, a thick
  black stroke and a black SE drop.

## Words

| Say | Not | For |
|---|---|---|
| Draw 1 card / Draw 2 cards | Draw 1 | every draw |
| extra | more, additional | an added amount: `SHOT +10% extra`, `Draw 1 extra card` |
| or more | or higher | a threshold: `75% or more` |
| or fewer | or less | a count: `1 card or fewer` |
| or less / less than | | a number or percentage: `Shot Clock 03 or less`, `SHOT less than 50%` |
| after | following | `after a Rebound` |
| target player | player of choice | who a card aims at |
| new player | receiver | whoever the pass reaches |
| You may … | | anything optional |
| while passing | | as possession changes, before the draw phase |
| possession | turn | a player's go. **Turn** is only ever rotating a card |
| it has | it gets | a boost that belongs to one shot only |

## Keywords

Capitalised and styled: Pass, Draw, Discard, Clear, Shot Clock, Rebound, Three, Force, Assign,
Reset, Turn, Score. A Special Move **is** a Move card wherever a card says "Move".

## Layout

- **Parentheses are clarification**, printed smaller and in italics:
  `(They do not play a Pass card)`.
- **Choices and outcomes go on lines of their own**: a lead line, then bullets.

  ```
  Flip a coin:
  • Heads: SHOT +25%
  • Tails: SHOT -25%
  ```

- **Anything not player-facing stays off the card** — Spazzphalt's roll, Equalizer's timing.

## Combos

- Shown as pictures; the only words are the combo's name and its payoff.
- **No "After a …"**: the double dotted chevrons show which card comes first.
- Printed on **every** card in the combo.
- A partner that is any card of a type (any dunk) is that type's icon.
- Until the combo has been pulled off, a circle with a question mark stands in for the partner.

## Vocabulary settled in the Move / Intangible audit

- **"Draw 1 extra card"**, never "Draw 1 card extra".
- **Clamps are assignments**, so the verbs are:
  - **Assign** — putting one on somebody. A defensive assignment.
  - **Reassign** — moving one that is already out.
  - **Clear** — taking one off.
- **"while passing"** means *after the pass is committed, before the ball changes hands*.
- **Retire** is for cards and officials. A Clamp is Cleared, not Retired.
- **~[Ref]** is the keyword in effect text, even on cards whose *name* says Official.
- **Force**, not Knock — pushing a card out of somebody's hand into the next player's.
